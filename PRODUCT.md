## Platform

web

## Users

Residential customers in Hobart, Tasmania, weighted equally in this first phase:

- Homeowners and households wanting regular or deep house cleaning.
- Tenants and property managers needing End of Lease cleaning before a vacate date.
- Airbnb and short-stay hosts needing reliable turnover cleaning.

Their job: find a trustworthy local cleaner, understand whether their need is covered, and request a tailored quote or make contact quickly, very often from a phone.

Secondary audiences (commercial/office clients, builders needing post-construction cleaning) are served by the site but are not the primary focus of phase 1.

## Product Purpose

GreenCoreCleaning is a pre-launch professional cleaning business in Hobart. The website exists to:

- Generate qualified leads and cleaning quote requests (primary conversion: **Get a Free Quote**).
- Increase phone and WhatsApp enquiries.
- Build visibility on Google Search and Maps for Hobart cleaning searches.
- Establish credibility and trust before the business has a track record.
- Eventually support online bookings.

Success: a first-time visitor understands what GreenCoreCleaning does, that it serves Hobart, and how to request a quote within seconds.

## Product Principles

1. Truth before persuasion: every claim on the site must be verifiable; a pre-launch business earns trust through clarity and professionalism, not invented proof.
2. The quote is the product's front door: every surface should make requesting a free quote obvious and easy, especially on mobile.
3. Local relevance without spam: speak to Hobart naturally; expand to suburbs only with genuinely useful, unique content.
4. One approved step at a time: the owner makes all final decisions; nothing is built, installed or published without explicit approval.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: accessible colour contrast (white text uses `#3F7D38`, not `#509948`, on buttons), keyboard navigation, accessible forms with clear labels and errors, reduced-motion support, minimum 44px touch targets, and strong mobile usability.
